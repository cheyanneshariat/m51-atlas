Scientific notes

This is a working atlas for source inspection, not a vetted catalog of confirmed single stars or super-Eddington objects. Public snapshot: 4 September 2026.

Samples and fits

The blue sample contains 26 previously selected sources. The warm sample contains 109 sources with ACS F606W − F814W colors from 0.25 to 1; the cool sample contains nine with colors greater than 1. Warm and cool objects were selected above the plotted color–magnitude projection of an adopted Humphreys–Davidson reference. Position above that curve alone does not establish a physical luminosity above the limit.

SED temperatures, luminosities, and extinction values are provisional model fits. Blending, extinction degeneracies, incomplete photometry, and model limits can affect them. The warm sample includes 24 compact-cluster catalog matches and 19 sources whose existing SED classification is below the adopted physical luminosity threshold; these groups may overlap. Sample membership is not a spectral classification.

Light-curve caveats

The large ACS F606W discontinuities in sources 20 and 189090 coincide with changes between separately reduced DOLPHOT chunks and are very likely reduction artifacts. They should not be interpreted as established outbursts. Other sources have not all received the same exposure-level audit. Passing photometric quality cuts does not validate variability, and smaller variations still require checking.

Images and photometry

Image panels use independent display stretches and are not flux comparisons between filters. A visible feature is not automatically a secure counterpart. In the cool sample, source 149689's matched WFC3 measurements fail the adopted cuts in all five filters; source 193's two UV measurements fail the crowding cut. The atlas images and existing fits are retained for inspection, not presented as newly verified measurements.

The public pages contain the atlas figures and PDF downloads. Internal working tables and reduction files are not included. For interpretation or quantitative use, please contact Cheyanne Shariat.

Blueward BSG candidates

Sources 364, 402, 343194, 343301, and 343380 are a separate five-source selection, not additions to the original 144-source sample or its overview CMD. BSG denotes a blue-supergiant candidate, not a confirmed spectral classification or merger origin.

The five-band atmosphere fits adopt a nonnegative half-normal internal extinction prior with sigma A_V = 0.2 mag at 7.50 Mpc, keeping the original atmosphere branch. Labels give posterior medians; model points show the grid MAP fit. Milky Way foreground corrections are retained. The CMD reference curves retain their original A_V = 0.62 assumptions; that value is not imposed on these fits. ACS variability and individual image registration have not been independently validated for this addition. Image cutouts preserve the catalog positions, 4-arcsecond fields, and 1-arcsecond scale bars.
